## 🌟 Overview

**12 Gifts of Christmas** is a heartwarming **Hallmark Channel** holiday movie that captures the spirit of giving, love, and rediscovering what truly matters during the Christmas season.  

- 🎬 **Director:** Peter Sullivan  
- 🕊️ **Genre:** Romance, Drama, Holiday  
- 📅 **Release Date:** November 26, 2015  
- 🏠 **Network:** Hallmark Channel  

---

## 🎄 Plot Summary

When **Anna**, a struggling artist, takes a job as a **personal Christmas shopper**, her life takes a turn she never expected.  
Her client, **Marc Rehnquist**, is a high-powered executive who doesn’t have time for the holidays.  
Through the process of choosing twelve meaningful gifts for the people in his life, Marc begins to rediscover the joy and warmth of Christmas — and maybe even love.

> 💬 *A simple job turns into a journey of connection, gratitude, and Christmas magic.*
